## Overview

**Hoàn Hời** is a cashback platform for Vietnamese shoppers. Before buying, users open a product through a Hoàn Hời link; the marketplace pays a commission for that order, and Hoàn Hời shares most of it back to the user's wallet.

I founded the product and built it end-to-end. It is live in production at [hoanhoi.com](https://hoanhoi.com).

## How it works

1. **Paste a product link** from a supported marketplace
2. **Shop as usual** through the generated tracked link
3. **Get cashback** in your wallet once the order is reconciled

## Features

- Multi-marketplace support: Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Alibaba, Klook, Pharmacity and more
- Single-link and bulk-link generation
- Cashback order tracking, wallet, created-link and click history
- Help center, policies, and support & dispute flow

## Tech

- Next.js, React
- Self-hosted in production
