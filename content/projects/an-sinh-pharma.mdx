## Product Data

Filled the WooCommerce catalogue with a Python crawler:

- Crawl product pages from pharmacy sitemaps and extract names, prices, descriptions, categories and images.
- Rebuild the category tree and import products and images into WooCommerce through its REST API, with parallel uploads for large batches.
