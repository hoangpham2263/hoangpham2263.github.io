Rank Math is the SEO plugin I use on most sites at Puramu. Installing it is easy, but many sites keep the defaults and miss a lot. These are my steps for every new site.

## 1. Setup wizard

- Choose **Advanced** mode.
- Set the site type, name and logo (used for Organization schema).
- Connect **Google Search Console**.

## 2. Only the modules you need

Usually: **Sitemap, Schema, Redirections, 404 Monitor (when needed), Image SEO, Breadcrumbs**.

## 3. Sitemap

- Include posts, pages, products and main categories.
- **Exclude** tags, author archives on single-author sites and minor content types.
- Submit `/sitemap_index.xml` to Search Console.

## 4. Title and description templates

```text
Posts:     %title% %sep% %sitename%
Products:  %title% - Best price %sep% %sitename%
```

Templates are a fallback — key pages still deserve hand-written titles and descriptions.

## 5. Noindex what shouldn't rank

Tag archives, author archives (single author), internal search; cart, checkout and account for shops.

## 6. Schema

Article/BlogPosting for posts, Product for WooCommerce, full LocalBusiness details for local businesses. Validate with Google's *Rich Results Test*.

## 7. Redirects and 404s

Use the Redirections module for 301s; 404 Monitor finds broken links — clear its log regularly.

## 8. Breadcrumbs

Enable and add them to the theme — good for users and for Google's understanding of structure.

## Mistakes I've seen

- Rank Math and Yoast together → duplicate meta and schema.
- Forgetting WordPress's "discourage search engines" option at launch.
- Themes printing their own schema/breadcrumbs → duplicates.
