Search behaviour is changing: instead of reading ten results, many people read an AI summary from Google's **AI Overviews / AI Mode**, or ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity directly. The question for site owners: how do you still get **mentioned and clicked**?

## What Google says

In *AI features and your website* on Google Search Central, Google explains that:

- AI Overviews and AI Mode show **links to websites** so people can dig deeper.
- These features may run several related searches ("query fan-out"), so they can surface a wider set of pages than classic results.
- **Core SEO best practices still apply** — there's no special trick beyond helpful content and a crawlable site.

In short: a solid SEO foundation is the foundation for appearing in AI answers.

## 7 things to do now

### 1. Answer directly and clearly
Open each service page or article with a direct answer to its main question, then go into detail. Use question-style H2/H3s, comparison tables and step lists — formats that are easy to cite.

### 2. Real, specific, sourced information
Numbers, price ranges, timelines, processes — specific, sourced content beats generic copy. Show when it was last updated.

### 3. Show expertise and the people behind it
About page, post authors, real projects, client reviews. For local businesses: consistent name, address and phone everywhere.

### 4. Structured data
Organization/LocalBusiness, Service, Product, Article, FAQ (only for genuine Q&A) and BreadcrumbList help machines understand exactly what a page is.

### 5. Fast, machine-readable pages
Main content in the HTML (not entirely JavaScript-dependent), good speed, and no accidental crawler blocks in `robots.txt`. See the [technical SEO checklist](/en/blog/seo-ky-thuat-website-moi).

### 6. Decide on AI crawlers
Besides Googlebot, many AI companies run their own crawlers. You can allow or block them in `robots.txt`: blocking makes it harder to appear in that tool's answers; allowing means your content can be cited but also used for summaries. Decide deliberately rather than by default.

### 7. Consider llms.txt
`llms.txt` is a **proposal** (by Jeremy Howard in 2024, spec at llmstxt.org): a Markdown file at your site root that summarises the site and lists key pages for language models. It is **not an official standard**, and how much major AI tools actually use it is unclear — but it's cheap to add:

```markdown
# ABC Company

> WordPress and WooCommerce websites for businesses in Ho Chi Minh City.

## Services
- [Web design](https://example.com/services/web-design/): business sites and stores
- [Maintenance](https://example.com/services/maintenance/): updates, backups, security

## Contact
- [Contact](https://example.com/contact/)
```

## How to measure

- **Search Console**: impressions and clicks from Google (appearances in Google's AI features are included in the Performance report).
- In Analytics, track referrals from AI tools (e.g. chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com).
- Ask AI assistants the questions your customers ask and see whether your site is mentioned.
